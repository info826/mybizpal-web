Our Mission

We built what we wished existed — an AI that answers every call, qualifies every lead, and books every appointment. Automatically. No voicemail. No missed opportunities. No expensive human SDRs burning through budget on calls that go nowhere.

The Problem We Solve

Most service businesses lose revenue before anyone even answers. A lead calls, hits voicemail, and calls a competitor. A sales team misses a follow-up because someone was in a meeting. A £500-a-day SDR spends half their time on calls that go nowhere.

These aren't technology problems. They're human-capacity problems. And AI is the answer — if it's built right.

Meet Your AI Revenue Agent

Our AI agent is not a chatbot. It's a fully conversational AI voice operator that picks up every call, understands the context, asks the right questions, and books qualified appointments — without scripts, without hold music, without lunch breaks.

The Technology

MyBizPal runs on a purpose-engineered AI stack designed for one thing: voice that feels human, at scale. Our system processes speech in real time with sub-second latency. Every conversation is structured, logged, and actionable — feeding your pipeline automatically.

We obsess over latency, reliability, and conversational quality. In sales, a half-second delay is the difference between a booked appointment and a dead call.
