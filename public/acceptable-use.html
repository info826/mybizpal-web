Acceptable Use Policy

Rules governing how MyBizPal may be used. Violations may result in immediate account suspension.

Last updated: 2 June 2025  |  Version: v1.0  |  {{COMPANY_NAME}}  |  Company No. {{COMPANY_NO}}

1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) governs all use of the MyBizPal Platform and all AI Agents deployed through it — including the inbound and outbound voice agent, WhatsApp and SMS messaging agent, and any custom AI Agents you create or configure. By using the Platform you agree to comply in full. This AUP is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Service.

2. Permitted Uses

You may use the Platform and any AI Agent for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Handling inbound telephone calls from prospective or existing customers via the voice agent
  • Running B2B Outbound Campaigns using voice calls and voicemail drops to Cold Contacts, subject to CTPS screening and channel rules
  • Sending SMS, WhatsApp, and email follow-ups to Warm Contacts and Consented Contacts
  • Sending B2B email to generic business addresses as a follow-up or cold outreach channel, with a functioning unsubscribe mechanism
  • Using Data Enrichment (Google Places API, Companies House API) to generate business lead lists
  • Qualifying business leads across any permitted channel
  • Booking appointments and managing your business calendar
  • Creating and deploying custom AI Agents for your own business operations
  • Integrating AI automation into legitimate sales, customer service, and business operations

3. Prohibited Uses

⚠ Zero Tolerance

The following uses will result in immediate account suspension without refund and may be reported to relevant authorities.

3.1 Illegal Calling & Messaging

  • Calling CTPS-registered numbers without valid consent or a prior business relationship
  • B2C cold calling without PECR-compliant consent
  • Sending unsolicited SMS or WhatsApp messages to Cold Contacts
  • Sending unsolicited email to named individuals without consent or a lawful basis under GDPR
  • Sending bulk commercial email without a functioning unsubscribe mechanism
  • Contacting any number or address on your DNC List
  • Making more than three contact attempts to a non-responsive Cold Contact
  • Contacting businesses or individuals outside permitted hours without explicit prior consent
  • Using Data Enrichment data to build, export, or sell standalone contact databases outside the Platform

3.2 Deception & Impersonation

  • Configuring any AI Agent to deny being an AI when sincerely asked — on any channel
  • Impersonating another business, brand, or individual via any AI Agent
  • Using false caller ID or misleading calling line identification on voice agents
  • Configuring a WhatsApp or SMS agent to misrepresent the sender identity
  • Making calls or sending messages on behalf of a third party without their knowledge and consent

3.3 Harassment & Abuse

  • Calling individuals who have asked not to be contacted
  • Using the platform to harass, intimidate, or threaten any person
  • Running campaigns designed to overwhelm or disrupt a target

3.4 Illegal Data Handling

  • Uploading lead data you do not have the right to use
  • Scraping or purchasing contact lists in violation of GDPR or applicable data laws
  • Processing special category data without explicit consent
  • Storing or processing children's data through the platform
  • Any use that violates GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, or equivalent legislation

3.5 EU AI Act Prohibited Practices

The following are prohibited under Article 5 of the EU AI Act:

  • Using any AI Agent to deploy subliminal techniques or manipulative methods that cause or are likely to cause harm
  • Exploiting the vulnerabilities of any person based on age, disability, or social or economic circumstances
  • Conducting or enabling social scoring of individuals based on their behaviour
  • Using any AI Agent for real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces
  • Inferring the emotions of individuals in workplace or educational settings without a lawful basis

3.6 Other Prohibited Activities

  • Any unlawful, fraudulent, or criminal activity
  • Attempting to reverse engineer or extract the underlying AI or technology
  • Reselling or sublicensing platform access without written consent
  • Overloading the platform with automated requests that degrade service for others
  • Any use that could bring MyBizPal into disrepute

4. Your Compliance Responsibilities

  • UK: PECR, UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, Ofcom regulations
  • EU: GDPR, national telecommunications laws
  • US: TCPA, CAN-SPAM, state-level privacy laws
  • Other: Applicable local telemarketing and data protection laws

We provide tools — you are responsible for how you use them. If unsure, seek legal advice before proceeding.

5. Consequences of Violation

  • Immediate suspension or termination without notice
  • No refund for current or remaining billing period
  • Data may be preserved and disclosed to meet legal or regulatory obligations
  • We reserve the right to pursue civil legal action for losses caused by your violation
  • Activities may be reported to the ICO, Ofcom, or law enforcement
