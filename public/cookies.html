1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember preferences, keep you logged in, and collect analytics to improve performance. They do not contain personal information by themselves but may be combined with other data.

4. Managing Cookies

A cookie consent banner appears on first visit. You can accept all, essential only, or customise. You can change preferences at any time by clicking the cookie icon in the bottom-left corner of the site.

Browser Cookie Settings
  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Blocking essential cookies may affect platform functionality.
