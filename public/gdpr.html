9. EU AI Act Compliance

The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) applies alongside UK GDPR for AI systems that interact with individuals. The Act has extraterritorial scope and applies to MyBizPal and to our clients where AI Agents interact with individuals in EU member states.

Risk Classification

All MyBizPal AI Agents are classified as Limited Risk AI Systems under Article 50 of the EU AI Act. They are not classified as high-risk systems under Annex III as currently constituted.

Article 50 Transparency (effective 2 August 2026)

All AI Agents are designed to identify as AI at the first point of every interaction. This is a mandatory design requirement that cannot be disabled by clients. Disclosure is delivered in the language of the conversation.

Article 4 AI Literacy (in force since 2 February 2025)

MyBizPal ensures that its staff, contractors, and partners who develop, operate, or manage AI systems have a sufficient level of AI literacy as required by Article 4. Clients are similarly obligated in relation to their use of the Platform.

Prohibited Practices (in force since 2 February 2025)

MyBizPal does not design, and prohibits clients from using, any AI Agent for practices prohibited under Article 5 of the EU AI Act, including subliminal manipulation, exploitation of vulnerabilities, social scoring, or real-time remote biometric identification.

Interaction with GDPR

The EU AI Act operates alongside, and does not replace, UK GDPR and EU GDPR obligations. Both frameworks apply concurrently. Transparency disclosures required under Article 50 of the AI Act complement but are separate from data subject information rights under GDPR Articles 13 and 14.
