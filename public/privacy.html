This data is used solely for the purpose of the Outbound Campaign it was generated for and is not sold or shared with third parties.

12. EU AI Act & Your Rights

The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) imposes transparency obligations on providers and deployers of AI systems. Where these obligations apply:

  • You have the right to be informed that you are interacting with an AI system at the very first point of contact
  • You have the right to request human intervention or escalation where appropriate
  • You have the right not to be subject to AI systems that use prohibited practices under the EU AI Act
  • You may lodge a complaint with the relevant national AI supervisory authority

MyBizPal has classified all its AI Agents as Limited Risk systems under Article 50 of the EU AI Act.
